How to play
  • Click the clock to earn ticks.
  • Machines make ticks every second, even when you are not clicking. Each one lights a numeral on the dial.
  • Upgrades multiply your clicks or your machines.
  • Perfect Tick: click while the marker is in the green zone. That click pays 2.5x, plus 0.5x for every Perfect in a row, up to 14.5x. Miss a beat for 6 seconds and the streak resets.
  • Fire: at 10K lifetime ticks the clock ignites. It burns hotter at 10M and turns blue at 1T.
  • Gems come from achievements, golden chimes, Perfect streaks and rewinds. Spend them in the Skins tab.
  • Rewind resets your run for gears, a permanent +5% production each.

How to earn gems
  • Achievements: 3 gems each.
